## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an editorial, cinematic, and authoritative identity for Lagos-based Graphics & Motion Designer Prince Anosike. The aesthetic centers on restrained luxury, precision craft, and dramatic scale contrasts. 

### Visual Tenets
- **Cinematic Restraint**: Deep nocturnal surfaces punctuated by ultra-fine lines and highly deliberate micro-moments of intense blue illumination.
- **Editorial Monumentality**: Vast, classic display type juxtaposed with sharp, technical modern sans interfaces.
- **Material Realism without Ornament**: Surfaces feel like dark museum slate, oiled paper, and calibrated reference monitors. No extraneous visual decoration.

### Anti-Vibe Directives
- **Zero Purple Gradients**: Strict ban on generative AI gradients, vaporwave hues, and multi-color fades.
- **Zero Glassmorphism**: No frosted backdrop-blurs, glowing drop shadows, or floating translucent containers.
- **Zero Decorative Frivolity**: No emojis, sparkles, floating geometric stickers, or generic 3-column icon grids.
- **Structural Framing**: Use 1px hairline dividers and strictly aligned grid lanes. Elevation must never exceed a 4px physical offset on hover.

## Colors

The palette is engineered with a strict hierarchy dominated by near-black ink and abyssal oceanic navies, reserving bright chromatic energy strictly for critical interactive anchors.

### Base Tiers
- **Base / Canvas**: `#050608` (Ink). The foundational background for all full-bleed pages and viewport frames.
- **Surface 1**: `#091B29` (Deep Navy). Structural panels, persistent navigation, section backdrops, and media enclosures.
- **Surface 2**: `#0F2A3F` (Navy Lift). Interactive card states, input fields, and hover boundaries.

### Accents & Semantics
- **Accent Primary**: `#02A9EF` (Signal Blue). Used sparingly (maximum 5% viewport real estate) for primary call-to-actions, active playback heads, and motion indicators.
- **Accent Pressed / Deep**: `#0287BF` (Signal Pressed). Active states, pressed buttons, and selected controls.
- **Success**: `#2ED47A` (Mint). Project status indicators and availability tags.
- **Error**: `#FF5C5C` (Coral). Form validation alerts and systemic warnings.

### Typography & Dividers
- **Text Primary**: `#F4F6F8` (Paper). Headlines, project titles, primary reading copy.
- **Text Secondary**: `#9AA7B2` (Mist). Metadata, technical specs, captions, and secondary actions.
- **Line / Hairline**: `#1B2B38`. Exactly 1px structural grid lines, dividers, and card borders.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional clash between the high-culture editorial authority of serif display letterforms and the precision, industrial clarity of a contemporary geometric sans.

### Font Pairing Rules
- **Display & Headings**: Rendered using a high-contrast serif typeface. Restricted strictly to weights 500 (Medium) and 600 (SemiBold). Applied tracking must remain tight (-1% to -2%). The serif face must **never** be rendered below 24px under any layout context.
- **Interface & Body**: Rendered in `Outfit`. Restricted to weights 400 (Regular) and 500 (Medium). `Outfit` must **never** be styled larger than 24px, with the sole exception of technical telemetry and numeric milestone counters (`stat-display`).
- **Labels & Micro-data**: All metadata markers, role identifiers, year indices, and section tags must be rendered uppercase with `+0.08em` tracking in `Outfit`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a disciplined 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content width of 1280px. Sectioning prioritizes expansive negative space to give graphics and motion reels cinema-grade breathing room.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns. Gutters: `16px` (`1rem`). Side canvas margins: `20px` (`1.25rem`). Section vertical separation: `64px` (`4rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns. Gutters: `24px` (`1.5rem`). Side canvas margins: `32px` (`2rem`). Section vertical separation: `96px` (`6rem`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns. Gutters: `24px` (`1.5rem`). Side canvas margins: dynamic centering up to `1280px` max-width container, with outer canvas padding scaling up to `80px` (`5rem`). Section vertical separation: `128px` (`8rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
Vertical flow relies exclusively on multiples of 8px. Dividers snap directly across column lines. Text containers in case studies must not exceed 7 grid columns (approx. 720px) to preserve optimal reading length.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely through flat surface layering, crisp 1px borders, and spatial contrast rather than atmospheric glow or heavy shadows.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Canvas Layer (`#050608`)**: Bottom layer for outer margins and primary canvas.
2. **Structural Tier (`#091B29`)**: Static content cards, project grids, pinned navigation panels, and drawer canvases.
3. **Interactive Tier (`#0F2A3F`)**: Active media cards, dropdowns, and form control fields.

### Outlines & Borders
No blurred shadows or colorful halos are permitted. Depth separation is executed through `1px solid #1B2B38` hairline outlines. 

### Hover & Motion Rules
Hover states rely on subtle chromatic lift and physical translation:
- Maximum hover displacement: `translateY(-4px)` with a strict `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing.
- Border highlight shifts from `#1B2B38` to `#02A9EF` at 50% opacity or `#0F2A3F` at 100% opacity.

## Shapes

The design uses deliberate corner radiuses mapped strictly by component taxonomy.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radius (2px)**: Video embeds, image thumbnails, media stills, metadata tags, and status chips. Preserves the crisp, filmic precision of media assets.
- **Card & Container Radius (12px)**: Interactive project cards, form inputs, modal dialogs, and flyout navigation panels.
- **Full Pill (999px)**: Action buttons, play triggers, contact triggers, and cursor indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Full roundedness (`999px`). Background `#02A9EF`, typography `Outfit 15px/Medium` in `#F4F6F8`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: background `#0287BF`, transform `translateY(-2px)`.
- **Ghost Editorial Pill**: Full roundedness (`999px`). Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #1B2B38`, text `#F4F6F8`. Hover: border `#02A9EF`, text `#02A9EF`, background `rgba(2, 169, 239, 0.05)`.

### Project Cards & Motion Showcases
- **Container**: `12px` border radius, background `#091B29`, border `1px solid #1B2B38`.
- **Media Port**: Inner media element has a strict `2px` radius and a `16:9` or `4:5` ratio.
- **State Changes**: On hover, elevate card by `translateY(-4px)`, shift border to `#0F2A3F`, and reveal project title in `#F4F6F8` with metadata in `#9AA7B2`.

### Tags & Chips
- **Geometry**: Border radius `2px`. Padding: `4px 8px`.
- **Style**: Background `#091B29`, border `1px solid #1B2B38`.
- **Typography**: Label MD (`Outfit 13px, Medium, Uppercase, +0.08em tracking`), color `#9AA7B2`. Status indicators use a 6px circular dot (`#2ED47A` for "Available for projects").

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: `12px` border radius. Background `#091B29`, border `1px solid #1B2B38`, text `#F4F6F8`. Placeholder `#9AA7B2`. Padding: `16px 20px`.
- **Focus State**: Border `#02A9EF`. No outer focus glow; use an internal crisp border transition.
- **Selection Controls**: Checkboxes and radios styled as custom square/circle elements with `1px solid #1B2B38`, filling with `#02A9EF` upon selection.

### Custom Motion Player Scrubbers
- **Track**: 2px height, `#1B2B38` base color.
- **Fill / Playhead**: `#02A9EF` progress line with a minimal 8px pill head, triggering hover timing indicators in `Outfit 13px Uppercase`.
